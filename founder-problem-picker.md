# Founder Problem Picker

Use this guide when you know the operating problem but do not know which kit to open.

| Problem | Symptoms | Use this kit | Input needed | Output generated | Underlying repo | Optional advanced automation path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I do not know which deals need attention | Silent proposals, old next steps, unclear owner follow-up, late decision timelines | [Revenue Rescue Kit](starter-kits/revenue-rescue-kit/README.md) | Deal stage, value, last activity, next step, owner, risk level | Stuck deal action plan | [founder-os-revenue-engine](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/founder-os-revenue-engine) | Route CRM or sheet signals into the Founder Ops Command Router after the manual action plan works |
| I need a weekly leadership review | Metrics are scattered, owners are unclear, risks are discussed too late | [Weekly Review Kit](starter-kits/weekly-review-kit/README.md) | Area, metric, current value, previous value, status, blocker, next action | Founder weekly operating review | [founder-weekly-operating-review-agent](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/founder-weekly-operating-review-agent) | Use n8n to assemble weekly signal summaries after the review format is trusted |
| I need to update investors | Highlights, metrics, risks, asks, and next month priorities take too long to draft | [Investor Update Kit](starter-kits/investor-update-kit/README.md) | Update section, item, current status, evidence, risk or ask, next step | Investor update draft | [board-pack-investor-update-agent](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/board-pack-investor-update-agent) | Connect validated metrics and review outputs into an investor update workflow |
| Customers are not completing onboarding | Closed-won accounts are slow to activate, blockers are unclear, owner follow-up is inconsistent | [Onboarding Risk Kit](starter-kits/onboarding-risk-kit/README.md) | Customer, plan value, onboarding stage, last touch, activation status, blocker, owner, risk level | Customer risk memo | [founder-customer-onboarding-os](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/founder-customer-onboarding-os) | Automate customer risk logs only after the team agrees on activation criteria |
| Candidates are stuck and hiring is slow | Candidates sit in stage too long, no owner action, unclear urgency, hiring decisions stay in founder memory | [Hiring Bottleneck Kit](starter-kits/hiring-bottleneck-kit/README.md) | Role, candidate, stage, days in stage, score, concern, next action, owner, urgency | Hiring bottleneck memo | [founder-hiring-talent-pipeline-os](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/founder-hiring-talent-pipeline-os) | Route candidate stage changes into action logs after the manual hiring review works |

The rule is simple: choose the kit that matches the decision you need this week, not the repo that sounds most complete.
