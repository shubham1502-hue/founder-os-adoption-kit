# Data Privacy Note

This repo is for operating design, not public data storage.

Do not put customer, prospect, employee, investor, borrower, merchant, payment, or internal company data into public forks.

Use private copies for real company data.

Use synthetic examples in public repos.

Remove or anonymize:

- emails
- phone numbers
- deal notes
- CRM IDs
- customer names
- prospect names
- employee names
- investor names
- financial details
- payment details
- internal company notes

The templates are meant to help you design a workflow. They are not a place to store private company data.

Before sharing a fork, check every CSV, markdown file, screenshot, and exported workflow for private information.
