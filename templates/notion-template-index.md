# Notion Template Index

You can recreate each kit as a simple Notion database and memo page.

| Notion database | Suggested fields | Matching kit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue rescue queue | Company, stage, value, last activity, next step, owner, risk | [Revenue Rescue Kit](../starter-kits/revenue-rescue-kit/README.md) |
| Weekly review tracker | Area, metric, current value, previous value, status, blocker, owner | [Weekly Review Kit](../starter-kits/weekly-review-kit/README.md) |
| Investor update tracker | Section, item, status, evidence, risk or ask, next step | [Investor Update Kit](../starter-kits/investor-update-kit/README.md) |
| Onboarding risk queue | Customer, stage, last touch, activation status, blocker, owner, risk | [Onboarding Risk Kit](../starter-kits/onboarding-risk-kit/README.md) |
| Hiring bottleneck queue | Role, candidate, stage, days in stage, score, concern, owner, urgency | [Hiring Bottleneck Kit](../starter-kits/hiring-bottleneck-kit/README.md) |

## Suggested setup

Create one private Notion database per active kit. Do not build the whole Founder OS in Notion on day one.
