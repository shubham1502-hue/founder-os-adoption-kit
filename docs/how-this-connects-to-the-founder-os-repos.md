## Adjacent repos

Use these when the operating problem expands beyond the starter kits:

- [founder-ai-workflow-roi-os](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/founder-ai-workflow-roi-os): decide what to automate, hire for, outsource, or keep manual
- [revops-infrastructure-playbook](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/revops-infrastructure-playbook): design CRM, stages, handoffs, reporting, and automations
- [startup-metrics-playbook](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/startup-metrics-playbook): define metrics before building reports
- [founder-outreach-tracker](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/founder-outreach-tracker): manage relationship follow-up
- [founder-retention-expansion-os](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/founder-retention-expansion-os): handle renewal, retention, expansion, and customer proof
- [founder-product-feedback-roadmap-os](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/founder-product-feedback-roadmap-os): turn customer signals into roadmap decisions
- [ai-gtm-command-center](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/ai-gtm-command-center): prepare outbound and account research
- [founder-led-sales-call-os](https://github.com/shubham1502-hue/founder-led-sales-call-os): learn from sales calls and rescue deals

## Adoption rule

Start with the kit. Move to the deeper repo only when you need more structure, repeatability, or operating detail.
