3. Decision owner
4. Urgency

Keep the output concise and ready for a founder hiring review.

Company context:
[Add hiring goals, role priorities, current team constraints, and evaluation rules.]

Hiring table:
[Paste CSV or table here.]

Constraints:
[Add confidentiality rules, founder time limits, score interpretation, or hiring process rules.]
