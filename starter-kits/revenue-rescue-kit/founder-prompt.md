# Founder Prompt: Revenue Rescue

```text
You are my Founder Ops assistant.

I need to identify stuck deals and revenue follow-up priorities.

Use the table below. Do not invent data. If information is missing, state what is missing.

For each deal, evaluate:
- deal value
- risk level
- last activity date
- whether the next step is concrete
- whether founder follow-up is needed

Return:
1. Top 3 deals needing attention
2. Revenue at risk
3. Recommended action for each deal
4. Follow-up message I can send
5. Owner
6. Deadline

Keep the output concise and execution-ready.

Company context:
[Add stage, ICP, sales motion, ACV, and current revenue goal.]

Deal table:
[Paste CSV or table here.]

Constraints:
[Add founder time, sales owner capacity, customer sensitivity, or investor-safe language constraints.]
```
