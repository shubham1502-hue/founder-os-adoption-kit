# Founder Prompt: Weekly Review

```text
You are my Founder Ops assistant.

I need a concise weekly operating review for a founder-led company.

Use the table below. Do not invent data. State assumptions and flag missing information.

Evaluate:
- wins
- risks
- metrics that changed materially
- blockers
- decisions needed
- owner actions
- next-week priorities

Return:
1. Wins
2. Risks
3. Decisions needed
4. Owner actions
5. Next-week priorities

Keep the output concise and ready for a founder review.

Company context:
[Add company stage, current strategy, team size, and top priorities.]

Weekly operating table:
[Paste CSV or table here.]

Constraints:
[Add meeting length, owner capacity, investor-safe language, or areas to exclude.]
```
